Sacraments & Services

Baptisms, weddings, memorials, and the other services of the Church. Here is what to expect from each one and how to arrange it.

Birth & Churching Baptism Chrismation Confession Unction Marriage Memorials Funeral

Birth, Naming & Churching Σαραντισμός

The Church marks a child's birth with prayers for mother and baby soon after the birth, in the hospital or at home. The child is named on the eighth day, and at 40 days mother and child come to church for the Churching service.

  • Right after birth: contact Fr. Rick to arrange a visit and prayers.
  • 40-Day Churching: contact the parish office to schedule. It can be on a Sunday or a weekday, whichever works for your family.

How to arrange: email Fr. Rick at frrick@ssppglenview.org or call the parish office at (847) 729-2235.

Baptism Βάπτισμα

Baptism joins a child, or an adult who was not baptized as an infant, to Christ and the Church. It is done by immersion in water and followed right away by Chrismation, the gift of the Holy Spirit. Families often celebrate afterward with relatives and friends.

  • Godparents (sponsors): at least one godparent is required and must be a baptized, practicing Orthodox Christian in good standing with the Church.
  • Scheduling: baptisms usually take place on Saturdays or after Sunday Liturgy. Parents meet with the priest to plan at least two months in advance.
  • What's needed: we'll give you a list of the items you'll need, along with documents to guide your preparations.
  • Adult baptism: adults coming to the faith for the first time usually spend about a year in instruction and preparation (the Catechumenate). That includes a catechism course, regular attendance at services, and individual guidance from the priest.

How to arrange: email Fr. Rick at frrick@ssppglenview.org at least two months before the date you have in mind.

Chrismation Χρίσμα

Chrismation receives someone already baptized in another Trinitarian Christian church into the Orthodox Church. They are anointed with the oil of Chrism, which gives the gift of the Holy Spirit.

  • Preparation: those preparing for Chrismation usually spend about a year in instruction (the Catechumenate), including a catechism course, regular attendance at services, and individual guidance from the priest.
  • Sponsor: a sponsor who is a practicing Orthodox Christian in good standing is required, as a godparent is at baptism.
  • Timing: catechumens are received during a Sunday Divine Liturgy on or near a major feast day, in front of the whole parish.

How to arrange: email Fr. Rick at frrick@ssppglenview.org to ask about the catechism class and next steps.

Confession Εξομολόγηση

Confession is a private sacrament with the priest: a chance to be honest before God about where you have fallen short, and to receive His forgiveness and guidance.

  • Preparing: this short guide walks through the Ten Commandments and the Beatitudes as a way to reflect beforehand. Come ready to talk about what weighs on you most.
  • When to come: Confession is available any time. Many people go during a fasting season, such as Great Lent, the Nativity Fast (Nov. 15 to Dec. 24), or the Dormition Fast (Aug. 1 to 15), since fasting and repentance go together. Most parishioners come at least once a year, and you can come as often as it helps.
  • Scheduling: Confession is by appointment with Fr. Rick or Fr. Peter. Contact either of them directly or call the parish office.
  • New to confession? It's fine to say so at the start. Many people preparing for Chrismation, or returning to the Church after time away, make their first confession as adults.
  • Confidentiality: what's said in confession is held in strict confidence.

How to arrange: call the parish office at (847) 729-2235 or email Fr. Rick at frrick@ssppglenview.org.

Holy Unction Ευχέλαιο

Holy Unction is anointing with blessed oil for the healing of body and soul. It is offered to anyone who is sick, facing surgery, or in need of God's strength and comfort.

  • Where: at the church or, when needed, at home or in the hospital.

How to arrange: call the parish office at (847) 729-2235 or email Fr. Rick at frrick@ssppglenview.org.

Marriage Γάμος

The Sacrament of Marriage unites a couple as husband and wife with the blessing of Jesus Christ. It is one of the most joyful services in the Orthodox Church, with the exchange of rings, the crowning, and the common cup.

  • Scheduling: contact the parish office at least one year before your intended date, and before you book a reception venue. Some dates are restricted by the Church calendar, including major feast days and fast periods. You will meet with the priest to discuss eligibility and begin planning.
  • Interfaith couples: we regularly celebrate weddings where one spouse is a baptized Christian who is not Orthodox. That spouse is not required to convert.
  • Koumbaro or Koumbara (sponsor): at least one sponsor must be a baptized Orthodox Christian in good standing with their local Greek Orthodox parish.
  • Pre-marital counseling: couples meet with the priest monthly for a year in a structured program.

When a marriage is struggling: please reach out early if you are facing serious difficulty, because counseling can make a real difference. If a marriage has ended in permanent separation and civil divorce, the Church asks that an ecclesiastical divorce be obtained before either spouse marries again in the Church. Contact Fr. Rick to talk through next steps.

How to arrange: call the parish office at (847) 729-2235 at least a year ahead of your date.

Memorials Μνημόσυνο

A memorial service can be held on a Sunday or another day. Memorials are traditionally offered at 40 days, 3 months, 6 months, 9 months, and one year after a death, and on each anniversary after that.

  • Kolyva: the boiled wheat shared at memorials is an important part of the tradition. You can make it at home (recipe) or order it from a local vendor.

How to arrange: call the parish office at (847) 729-2235 to schedule.

Funeral Κηδεία

Funerals are best planned well ahead of time, with a funeral home and the parish. When a death is sudden, the parish will help you through each step.

  • If a loved one has just died: call the parish office right away, any day of the week, at (847) 729-2235. Dial 2 for after-hours emergencies. We work closely with the funeral home and will guide you through planning the service.
  • Traditions: read our guidelines on Orthodox Christian funeral traditions.

How to arrange: call the parish office at (847) 729-2235.
