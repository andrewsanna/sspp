Beyond the Offering: Our Parish in Action

Stewardship is more than a line item on a budget sheet—it is our faith brought to life through action, sacrifice, and generosity. Here is a glimpse of what our parish family accomplished together this past year:

  • 1,500+ Hours of Hospitality
    Volunteers logged over 1,500 hours serving our neighbors and welcoming our broader community at this year's Greek Festival.
  • $115,000+ Given in Charity
    Our Philoptochos chapter donated over $115,000 to support families in crisis and charitable causes both locally and worldwide.
  • 100% Volunteer-Led Religious Education
    Our Church School runs entirely on the dedication of volunteer teachers, passing on the Orthodox faith to our children without a single paid staff position.
  • Care for Our Sacred Home
    A dedicated team of parishioner volunteers quietly maintains our church building and grounds every month, preserving our sanctuary at no cost to the parish.

Yes. Many parishioners choose to support the parish through non-cash giving options:

  • Appreciated Securities: Donating stock directly can help avoid capital gains taxes while providing a full fair-market-value tax deduction.
  • Retirement Account Distributions: Naming the parish as a beneficiary or utilizing a Qualified Charitable Distribution (QCD) can reduce estate and income tax burdens.
  • Life Insurance Policies: Transferring ownership or listing the parish as a beneficiary on an unused policy.
  • Property Gifts: Real estate or other assets can also be designated to support the long-term mission of the church.

Because tax laws vary, we recommend consulting your tax or financial advisor. Our parish office is happy to facilitate any of these gifts—please give us a call at (847) 729-2235.
