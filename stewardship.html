Stewardship

Stewardship is our way of saying thank you.

Everything we have is a gift from God — our time, our talents, and our resources. Stewardship is simply how we offer a portion of that back, so our parish family can keep worshiping, growing, and welcoming others home.

Give Now

"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."

2 Corinthians 9:7
More than money

Stewardship takes many forms

Time

Teaching Church School, greeting visitors, helping at the Festival, or singing in the choir — your presence is a gift in itself.

Talent

Whatever you're good at — hospitality, teaching, tech, hands-on repairs — there's a place for it here.

Treasure

There's no set amount and no obligation — just a prayerful gift of whatever you're able to offer, given freely.

Where it goes

Your generosity in action

Worship & Facilities

Keeping our sanctuary, parish hall, and grounds maintained for worship and community life.

Ministries & Youth

Supporting GOYA, Greek School, Church School, and every ministry that helps our community grow.

Outreach & Care

Funding Philoptochos philanthropy, pastoral care, and support for parishioners in need.
