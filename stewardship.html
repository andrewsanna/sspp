Stewardship

Stewardship is our way of saying thank you.

Everything we have is a gift from God — our time, our talents, and our resources. Stewardship is simply how we offer a portion of that back, so our parish family can keep worshiping, growing, and welcoming others home.

Yes. Beyond cash, check, or card, some stewards choose to give through:

  • Securities — donating appreciated stock can avoid capital gains tax while providing a full fair-market-value deduction.

  • Life insurance — an unneeded policy can be gifted directly to the parish.

Other ways to give

More ways to support the parish

  • Recurring giving — Set up weekly or monthly stewardship through the Realm portal, the same link above.
  • By mail — Checks can be mailed or dropped off to the parish office at 1401 Wagner Rd, Glenview, IL 60025.
  • In person — Drop off your offering at the parish office during regular hours.
  • Questions — Call (847) 729-2235; we're always happy to help with stewardship or planned giving.
  • Make a pledge — Commit your annual stewardship ahead of time using our pledge card.
