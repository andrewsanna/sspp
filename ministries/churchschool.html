Church School

Every Sunday, our Church School gathers to grow in faith together — from our youngest Pre-K learners to our graduating seniors — right after Divine Liturgy and Holy Communion.

Register for Church School

Church School begins with family worship in Divine Liturgy at 9:30 a.m. on Sunday mornings. Students proceed to class following Holy Communion, with classes running until 11:45 a.m. Our program serves children from Pre-K through 12th grade, with age-appropriate classes that grow with your child — from early lessons in the stories and traditions of our faith to deeper study of Orthodox theology and life as they get older.

Beyond the classroom, our Church School community comes together throughout the year for special feast day celebrations, service projects, and family events that bring the faith to life outside of Sunday mornings. We also rely on numerous volunteers — teachers, classroom aides, and helping hands for events — so if you're looking for a meaningful way to serve, this is a wonderful place to start.

"Watching my kids run to their classrooms every Sunday, excited to learn about their faith — that's when you know a program is doing something right."

— A Church School Parent
