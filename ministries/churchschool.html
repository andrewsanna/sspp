A joyful start to every Sunday.

Church School begins with family worship in Divine Liturgy, and continues with classes for every age, from Pre-K through 12th grade.

Grades Served
Pre-K – 12th
Divine Liturgy
9:30 AM Sunday
Classes Dismiss
11:45 AM

Classes begin with family worship in Divine Liturgy at 9:30 a.m. on Sunday mornings. We welcome all students to proceed to class following Holy Communion. Our knowledgeable and dedicated teachers are committed to inspiring students to grow their faith in Christ through age-appropriate curriculum and activities each week, fostering an environment for students to form lasting connections with their Orthodox classmates.

Classes end at 11:45 a.m. All students 3rd grade and younger must be picked up by a parent or older sibling.

Church School events

Saints Peter and Paul Church School offers a variety of events and participation opportunities for students, with the goal of deepening their faith and bringing Christ's teachings into their everyday lives.

During Lent

Project Hope Food Drive

"Brown Bag Sunday": our Lenten project that sets the example of giving.

Details

During Lent, the Church School takes part in this Lenten project to set the example of giving during this season of reflection.

Volunteer for this event
  • Serve as the main point of contact with the recipient organization
  • Create the list of goods needed
  • Distribute bags to classrooms ahead of time
  • Coordinate collecting the bags and delivering them to the recipient organization
Christmas Season

Christmas Pageant

Our wonderful cast of children brings the parish together to celebrate the season.

Details

This event is vital to bringing our community together to celebrate the season with our wonderful cast of children. We have a coordinator and need enthusiastic supporters.

Volunteer for this event
  • Costumes and sets
  • Casting: the sign-up and role assignment process for the children
  • Day-of oversight and logistics
Grades 7–12

Oratorical Festival

Students express their faith through speech, essay, song, chanting, or iconography.

Details

Our annual Oratorical Festival gives students in 7th–12th grades a chance to delve into their faith and express their learning through speech, essay, song, chanting, or iconography.

Volunteer for this event
  • Coordinators assist with promotion, registration, and selecting judges
  • Judge a category (judges cannot have family members participating)
  • Coordinate documents to align with Metropolis rules and submit them by strict deadlines

How you can help

Many volunteers are needed to make each program successful, so we ask families to consider volunteering for one or more Church School events. Sign-up takes place annually during Church School registration. Your service to the Church School program is an example to our children and a support to our community.

Substitute Teacher

Step in when one of our usual teachers must miss a week.

Details

We always need a bench of substitute teachers to make Sundays run smoothly. Lessons are planned, and you generally have a supporting role alongside the other teacher who is present. It's a chance to make a meaningful and direct impact.

Requirements
  • Mandatory youth safety training
  • Background check

Coffee Hour

Help host Church School Coffee Hour for the parish.

Details
  • Set up and clean up
  • Serve during coffee hour
  • Organize the SignUpGenius list for parents to bring food items

Sunday Bagels

Be a friendly face as students break their communion fast.

Details

Each Sunday, your children break their communion fast with mini bagels.

  • Pick up the bagels from their delivery spot and get them to the atrium
  • Be a friendly face passing them out

Photographer

Capture group shots on our major event days.

Details

Perks include getting the shots of your children from front and center!

  • Capture group shots on days of major events
  • Save them to a shared file
  • Coordinate with the Office to share them in the bulletin and on social media
