A class for every age

The Doves & The Fishermen

2-Year-Old & Turning-3 Classes · Mon–Thu or Mon–Fri, 8:45–11:30 AM

A gentle introduction to preschool with a 1-to-5 teacher ratio: daily socializing, songs, stories, and hands-on exploration of colors, shapes, letters, and numbers.

The Disciples

3-Year-Old Class · Mon–Fri, 8:45 AM–1:00 PM

Building on the social foundation from Fishermen, with more structured learning: letters, numbers 1–30, shapes, fine motor skills, and special Music, Art, and Greek Conversation classes.

The Apostles

Pre-K (4-Year-Old) Class · Mon–Fri, 8:45 AM–2:00 PM

Kindergarten-readiness through daily Free-Choice Centers, phonics and early reading, counting to 100, and special Greek Conversation and "Come and See" time with our parish priest.

The Saints

Kindergarten · Mon–Fri, 8:45 AM–3:00 PM

A full-day kindergarten curriculum comparable to neighboring districts, with Greek Language, Art, Gym, Music, Cooking, Orthodox Instruction, and weekly "Come and See" with our parish priest.

AGAPE is a nut-free early childhood center, sensitive to food allergies.

Our History

In 2000, upon approval of the Ss. Peter and Paul Parish Council and the support of Father Angelo Artemas, AGAPE Christian Academy was created. The idea of a Preschool began with the love and support of Carrie and John Colis along with five other founders including Elizabeth Cox and Julia Mantice. The founders worked together to plan, organize, and develop an Orthodox Christian Preschool that would nurture the spiritual, emotional, cognitive, and physical growth of young Orthodox Christians.

AGAPE opened its doors to the public on September 4th, 2001. Upon the creation of the preschool, the staff ensured that the school was certified and approved by DCFS. Back in 2001 when the preschool first began, it only offered two programs: the Disciple or 3-Year-Old Class and the Apostle or 4-Year-Old Class. Over the past twenty years, AGAPE has added classes for two-year-old children which is referred to as the Doves and Fishermen Class. We have also extended the school day for the 3-year-old and 4-year-old students to 1 o’clock or 2 o’clock pm. Some of the other additions through the years have been Greek, cooking, music, and art instruction, and Come and See Time with the parish priest, Greek instruction. Throughout AGAPE Preschool’s existence, it is proud to have grown and adapted to fit the ever-changing needs of its community.
