The Saints Peter and Paul Greek School is committed to an excellent educational program in a child-centered, child-friendly environment where learning is fun.

Our goal is to give students the skills to communicate in Greek, with an emphasis on conversation, while introducing them to Greek culture, civilization, and traditions. We also teach them to be kind, respectful, and faithful.

With a small number of students in each level, our teachers can focus on each child's individual needs and help them discover their abilities, so our students grow in confidence and self-respect.

What students learn

Preschool

First Greek words through songs, games, and stories: greetings, colors, numbers, family, and everyday objects.

Kindergarten – 2nd grade

The Greek alphabet, reading and writing simple words, and everyday conversation: introducing yourself, asking questions, and talking about home and school.

3rd – 4th grade

Reading short stories, building vocabulary, basic grammar, and writing simple sentences and short paragraphs.

5th – 6th grade

Reading comprehension, grammar and verb tenses, short compositions, and confident conversation, along with Greek history, geography, and mythology.

Every level, all year

Greek holidays and traditions, songs and poems, prayers in Greek such as the Lord's Prayer (Πάτερ ημών), and preparing for our Christmas, Independence Day, and End of Year programs.
