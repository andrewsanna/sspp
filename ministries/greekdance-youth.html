Our heritage, in every step.

Youth Greek Dance brings our young people together to celebrate our rich cultural heritage through traditional music and dance.

Grades
Kindergarten – 10th
Groups
3
Practice
Weekly

Dancers meet weekly by age group to learn regional dances from across Greece, build confidence, and forge lifelong parish friendships. Throughout the year, our groups perform at parish celebrations, local festivals, and community events. No experience needed. Every dancer starts somewhere, and our directors will meet your child where they are.

Three groups, one family

Each group is named in Greek and learns dances suited to its age, building skills year over year.

Kindergarten – 3rd Grade

Asterakia

“Little Stars”

Our youngest dancers learn the basics: rhythm, simple steps, and how to move together in the circle, all while having a great time with their friends.

DirectorCathy Gorecki
4th – 6th Grade

Elpida

“Hope”

Dancers build on the fundamentals with longer sequences and dances from more regions of Greece, and start taking on bigger roles in performances.

DirectorAnna Andrews
7th Grade & Up

Astrapi

“Lightning”

Our oldest dancers take on more challenging regional repertoire and lead the line at our biggest performances, setting the example for the groups coming up behind them.

DirectorAnna Andrews
