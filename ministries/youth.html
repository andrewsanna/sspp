Our groups

Elementary

Faith, Love & Joy (FLJ)

Faith, Love & Joy is where our youngest parishioners start building a real relationship with Christ and His Church. They meet one night a month for games, crafts, and lessons.

Monthly Gathering
Usually the 3rd Friday, 6:30–8:00pm

Dinner together (parents welcome, pizza's on us) · A theme night with three rotations: Orthodox Life Lesson, Arts & Crafts, and Gym Time

6th–8th Grade

Jr. GOYA

Jr. GOYA gives our 6th–8th graders a place to grow together in faith through worship, fellowship, outreach, and education. There are four different ways to get involved every month, so there's always something on the calendar.

Meeting
Usually the 2nd Monday, 6:30–8:00pm

Dinner, new & old business, a session on the year's theme, and time in the gym

Outing
Time & day vary each month

Retreats, ice skating, lock-ins & more

Friday Funday
Usually the 4th Friday, 6:30–9:00pm

Dinner, open gym, movies, bonfires

High School

Sr. GOYA

Sr. GOYA is where our high schoolers grow in faith together and figure out what that faith looks like in real life, through worship, fellowship, outreach, and honest conversation.

Meeting
Usually the 1st Monday, 6:30–8:00pm

Dinner, new & old business, a session on the year's theme

Outing
Day & time vary each month

Retreats, ice skating, lock-ins & more

CoffeeTalk
Day & time vary each month

Real talk over coffee. GOYAns pick the topics, and nothing's off-limits.
