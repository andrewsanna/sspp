Youth & Young Adults

Youth

From 1st grade through high school, our youth ministries give kids and teens a place to grow in faith, build lifelong friendships, and serve others — through weekly and monthly gatherings built around worship, fellowship, outreach, and education.

Faith, Love, & Joy (FLJ)
Elementary Age

Faith, Love & Joy (FLJ) aims to be a space of growth toward, familiarity with, and deepening of our elementary-age children's relationship with Christ and His Church — accomplished through intentional gatherings of fellowship, education, and service.

Monthly Gathering
Usually the 3rd Friday of the month, 6:30–8:00pm
  • Dinner together (parents welcome — complimentary pizza & refreshments)
  • A theme for the night, explored through a three-part rotation:
  • Orthodox Life Lesson
  • Arts & Crafts (tied to the theme)
  • Gym Time

Questions? Contact youthdirector@ssppglenview.org

Jr. GOYA
Junior High · 6th–8th Grade

Jr. GOYA aims to be a conduit for the holistic growth of our 6th–8th graders toward Christ and His Church, through intentional gatherings of worship, fellowship, outreach, and education. We meet four times a month so there's always a way to get involved.

Meeting
Usually the 2nd Monday of the month, 6:30–8:00pm
  • Dinner together
  • New & old business
  • 30–40 minute session on the year's theme
  • Activities in the gym
Outing
Time & day varies each month
  • Retreats, ice skating, lock-ins & more
Outreach
Time & day varies each month
  • Feed My Starving Children
  • Creating & distributing Blessing Bags
  • Visiting the Greek American Nursing Home
Friday Funday
Usually the 4th Friday of the month, 6:30–9:00pm
  • Dinner, open gym, movies, bonfires

Questions? Contact youthdirector@ssppglenview.org
