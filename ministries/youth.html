Our groups

Elementary

Faith, Love & Joy (FLJ)

Faith, Love & Joy is where our youngest parishioners start building a real relationship with Christ and His Church. They meet twice a month for games, crafts, and lessons.

Gathering
Usually the 1st Friday & 3rd Wednesday, 6:00–7:30pm

Dinner together (parents welcome, pizza's on us) · A theme night with three rotations: Orthodox Life Lesson, Arts & Crafts, and Gym Time

Parents Night
Quarterly Friday Gatherings

While the kids enjoy FLJ at church, parents of PreK–5th graders are invited to a nearby restaurant for dinner, fellowship, and discussion on raising kids in the church.

6th–8th Grade

Jr. GOYA

Jr. GOYA gives our 6th–8th graders a place to grow together in faith through worship, fellowship, outreach, and education. Designed specifically for middle schoolers, it helps youth build healthy friendships, serve others, and discover how their faith connects to their everyday lives.

Meeting
3x a month · 2nd & 4th Friday, 3rd Thursday, 6:30–8:00pm

Dinner, new & old business, a session on the year's theme, and time in the gym

Outreach
Monthly

Feed My Starving Children, Blessing Bags & more
High School

Sr. GOYA

Sr. GOYA is where our high schoolers grow in faith together and discover what that faith looks like in real life. Through worship, fellowship, outreach, and honest conversations, high schoolers dive deeper into Orthodoxy and tackle real-world issues. It’s a space where teenagers build lasting Christian friendships, grounding them today and equipping them with a foundation of faith for college and beyond.

Meeting
Weekly · 1st Tuesday, 2nd Wednesday, 3rd Friday, 4th Wednesday, 7:00–8:30pm

Dinner, new & old business, a session on the year's theme

CoffeeTalk
Day & time vary each month

Real talk over coffee. GOYAns pick the topics, and nothing's off-limits.

Serving others

Outreach is part of every month for our Jr. and Sr. GOYAns.

  • Feed My Starving Children

    Packing meals for children in need around the world.

  • Blessing Bags

    Assembling bags of everyday essentials for neighbors experiencing homelessness.

  • Visits to the Greek American Nursing Home

    Spending time with residents, from conversation to holiday visits.
