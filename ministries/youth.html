Youth Groups

Growing in faith, together.

From first grade through high school, our youth ministries give kids and teens a place to grow in faith, build lasting friendships, and belong.

Whether it's Friday night crafts, a Monday meeting, or a weekend away at retreat — our kids and teens always have somewhere to belong. Take a look at what's happening in each age group below.

Faith, Love, & Joy (FLJ)
Elementary Age

Faith, Love & Joy is where our youngest parishioners start building a real relationship with Christ and His Church — one fun-filled night a month, mixing games, crafts, and lessons that stick.

Monthly Gathering Usually the 3rd Friday, 6:30–8:00pm
  • Dinner together (parents welcome — pizza's on us)
  • A theme night with three rotations: Orthodox Life Lesson, Arts & Crafts, and Gym Time
Jr. GOYA
Junior High · 6th–8th Grade

Jr. GOYA gives our 6th–8th graders a place to grow together in faith — through worship, fellowship, outreach, and education. There are four different ways to get involved every month, so there's always something on the calendar.

Meeting Usually the 2nd Monday, 6:30–8:00pm
  • Dinner, new & old business, a session on the year's theme, and time in the gym
Outing Time & day vary each month
  • Retreats, ice skating, lock-ins & more
Outreach Time & day vary each month
  • Feed My Starving Children
  • Blessing Bags
  • Visits to the Greek American Nursing Home
Friday Funday Usually the 4th Friday, 6:30–9:00pm
  • Dinner, open gym, movies, bonfires
Sr. GOYA
High School

Sr. GOYA is where our high schoolers grow in faith together and figure out what that faith looks like in real life — through worship, fellowship, outreach, and honest conversation.

Meeting Usually the 1st Monday, 6:30–8:00pm
  • Dinner, new & old business, a session on the year's theme
Outing Day & time vary each month
  • Retreats, ice skating, lock-ins & more
Outreach Day & time vary each month
  • Feed My Starving Children
  • Blessing Bags
  • Visits to the Greek American Nursing Home
CoffeeTalk Day & time vary each month
  • Real talk over coffee — GOYAns pick the topics, nothing's off-limits
