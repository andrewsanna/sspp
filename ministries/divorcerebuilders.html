Support Groups

You don't have to walk through this alone.

For over fifteen years, Divorce Rebuilders has offered a place for the separated and divorced in our parish community to find support, guidance, and hope, grounded in our Orthodox faith.

Divorce Rebuilders is open to anyone in our community navigating separation or divorce — whether you were married in the Church or not, and whether you're Orthodox or not. Through instruction, guidance, and the support of others who understand what you're going through, our group offers a space to process the many aspects of the divorce experience without judgment.

Reach Out Confidentially
We keep meeting details private out of respect for everyone's confidentiality. Reach out to our Group Leader below and we'll share current meeting times and location directly with you.
