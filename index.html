Between Sundays

Most of parish life happens during the week. Philoptochos raises money and volunteers for families in need across Chicago. Sr. GOYA visits seniors at local nursing homes. Before Holy Friday, volunteers spend hours decorating the Epitaphios with flowers.

Youth & GOYA

A group for every age, from HOPE (ages 5 to 8) through Jr. and Sr. GOYA to the Young Adult League. Sr. GOYA meets four times a month for fellowship, outings, and service.
